Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026 | Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

GlayVue ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you" or "user"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and safeguarded when you use the GlayVue iOS application, 3D LiDAR scanning tools, GlazeLog recipe manager, Potters Hub social network, and related website services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our ceramic studio software. The types of data collected include:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, avatar image, and authentication credentials managed securely via Supabase.
  • LiDAR 3D Scan Data: 3D spatial meshes and point cloud data captured using your device's LiDAR camera. 3D scans are processed on-device and stored locally or uploaded to your private cloud storage when enabled.
  • Glaze Recipe & Studio Data: Ceramic glaze formulations, cone levels, test tile images, firing schedules, and studio inventory notes.
  • User Content: Posts, test tile pictures, comments, likes, and messages submitted to the Potters Hub community.
  • Technical Diagnostic Data: Device model, iOS version, anonymous crash logs, and app performance telemetry.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To authenticate user sessions and synchronize your glaze library across iOS devices.
  • To render real-time Metal shader glaze effects on 3D pottery meshes.
  • To enable community features such as sharing recipes, commenting, and following ceramic artists.
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To prevent fraudulent behavior, abuse, and illegal activity within the community.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or 3D scans to third parties for advertising or commercial marketing. Data is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate GlayVue:

  • Supabase: For secure database hosting, user authentication, and encrypted data storage.
  • Apple iCloud & App Store Services: For managing in-app subscriptions and on-device data syncing.

5. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

Under Apple App Store guidelines and global data privacy regulations (GDPR / CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access, review, or export your glaze recipes and 3D mesh files at any time.
  • Modify or update your profile details inside the GlayVue App settings.
  • Request Complete Account Deletion: You can delete your account and remove all personal data, posts, recipes, and 3D scans permanently by selecting "Delete Account" in Profile Settings or by emailing support@glayvue.app.

6. Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption for all network transmissions and AES-256 encryption at rest to protect your ceramic studio data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact our privacy compliance team at:

Email: support@glayvue.app
