Quick Troubleshooting Guides

LiDAR 3D Scan Optimization

Ensure your ceramic piece is placed on a matte surface with well-lit ambient room light. Avoid transparent glass turntables or direct intense glare while scanning.

Recipe Cloud Syncing

If your GlazeLog entries are not updating across your iPhone and iPad, pull down to refresh on the GlazeLog tab or check your internet connection.

Subscription Restoration

Purchased GlayVue Pro on a new iPhone? Navigate to My Studio Shelf → Settings → Restore Purchases to activate your Apple ID entitlements.
